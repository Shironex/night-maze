# A round has a quiet music bed, a chase layer and a sting, all recordings in the music group
Status: accepted (2026-10-16). Earlier: music only on the main menu (menu-music), and every sound and piece computed by a script (audio).
Code: src/game/RoundMusic.hpp, src/game/RoundMusic.cpp, src/game/NightMazeApp.cpp, src/audio/AudioEngine.hpp, src/game/StartOptions.hpp, assets/audio/README.md

Context: a night had only the wind and the cues. I generated four pieces in ElevenLabs under my commercial plan (solo piano, low strings, soft wordless choir): a second menu theme, a bed for the maze, a layer for the chase and a short sting for a won night.
Decision: the bed plays from 4 s into a round, fades in over 6 s and out over 1.5 s for the pause, a catch and the win. The chase layer follows the hunt of the shade (Shade::hunt, not shadeState, so the lamp does not flap it): in over 2 s, out over 4 s, and the bed is ducked to 0.4 under it. The sting plays once 1.5 s after a win and fades when its screen is left. All three are in the music group. `--menu-theme v2` loads the second theme into the place of the first, so the two can be compared; classic stays the default.
Why: the levels sit under the wind and the shade (bed -30 LUFS, chase -27, sting and themes -24), so the music colours the night and does not carry it. A settings field for the theme lost, as for the intro style: the comparison is temporary and must not touch the settings file.
Cost and revisit: the three pieces are decoded at the start, 49.9 MB of memory (the v2 theme 41.5 MB instead of 27.9 when chosen). menu-music said I would stream a second long piece; I kept decoding because the loops were cut short (48 s and 52 s), and I stream if more music comes. I drop one menu theme once I have chosen.
