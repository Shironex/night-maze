# The village is drawn with the sky but with real depth, and a rising camera shows it after a won night
Status: accepted (2026-10-09)
Code: src/game/Village.hpp, src/game/Village.cpp, src/game/VillageRenderer.cpp, src/game/Campaign.hpp, src/game/GameState.hpp, src/game/NightMazeApp.cpp, tools/blender/build_village.py, assets/ui/round_end.rml, tests/VillageTests.cpp

Context: The night end lines say how far the light has reached ("The near lamps are lit.", "The lane is lit as far as the well."), and nothing on the screen showed it. The village the lamps are for was a word only.
Decision: The village stands on a ridge, the far rim of the hollow, on the side opposite to the gate, so walking through the gate is walking towards it. The model is built around the eye, 257 m out with the lane 27 m up, and drawn like the sky, with the view matrix without its translation, scaled about the eye until its furthest vertex lies at 0.9 of the far plane. Unlike the sky it writes depth, so walls, hills and the gatehouse hide it. It is drawn with the textured program, with no light: pale walls and dark roofs come from the two halves of `lantern.png`, under one dim tint. Every won night adds one small model of light (5, 7, 8, 11 and 1 windows and lamp heads), amber and left to the bloom, so the draw calls are 1 plus the nights lit. A night shows the nights before it and its result adds its own, free play and the menus show the progress of the campaign, the intro shows none, and a tool run without `--night` shows none. On the result screen of a night the camera rises in 3.5 s to 5 m over the exit cell, turns to the ridge and narrows its lens to 22 degrees. The card moves to the right, the dim layer clears on the left, and the new lights come on as the camera arrives. After night 5 the same move runs for 7.5 s without a card (`GameMode::VillageBeat`), with the last window, then fades into the ending card.
Why: In the corridors the village is a rare glimpse, and that is accepted. With the eye at 1.7 m and walls of 3 m, the cap of the tower (9 degrees up) clears a wall only from about 8 m back and the lane (6 degrees) from about 12 m, and the houses are about 110 pixels wide in a window of 1280. So the reveal is what makes the feature seen: there the houses are about 330 pixels wide. I rejected painting the village into the sky pictures: it could not light up night by night and no wall would hide it. A real model at 257 m would lie outside the far plane of 100 m.
Cost and revisit: The village never comes closer and has no parallax. Only the faces that look at the eye exist, and the top of the ridge with the lane on it is never seen, so the lamp posts mark the lane. A window is 0.9 by 1.1 m, larger than a real one, and stands 0.3 m off its wall, because of the depth buffer at that distance. The ridge was first a strip 200 m wide that ended beside the village, with its foot 16 degrees below the horizon: the fog on the ground hid its ends and its foot from an eye in the corridors, but the model rides with the eye, so a raised camera (the menu glide, noclip) lifted them out of that fog and the hill stood in the sky as a black trapezoid. Now the ridge is the rim of the hollow, the whole way round the eye with its upper edge about 1 degree above the horizon, and its foot lies 45 degrees down, in the fog for every eye up to 60 m high (only noclip flies higher), so there is no end and no lower edge to see, and the hill of the village is the same as before. The shadow of night 3 does not stand at the end of the lane. The recorded menu video does not show the village until it is recorded again. I reopen it if the far plane changes or play moves to high places.
