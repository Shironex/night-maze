# Stone sheep stand in free corners and dead ends and look towards the gate
Status: accepted (2026-10-15)
Code: src/game/StoneSheep.hpp, src/game/StoneSheep.cpp, src/game/MazeWorld.cpp, src/game/MazeRenderer.cpp, src/game/Grass.cpp, tools/blender/build_stone_sheep.py, tools/blender/make_textures.py, tests/StoneSheepTests.cpp

Context: The story says the flock went to stone with the hedge and faces home. A maze had nothing that points at the gate except the chalk hints, which have to be found and read one by one.
Decision: A maze gets 3 stone sheep, 4 from 16 by 16 cells on and 5 from 22 by 22 on, in every mode. Each looks along the straight line from its cell to the exit cell, snapped to eight directions by the rule of the chalk hints (`compassTowards`). They are placed last, from a generator of their own, in dead ends and corner cells that hold nothing else: not the start, the exit or the cell before the gate, no crystal, lever, note, slab ring or puddle, not the heartstone (`MazeWorld::heartstone`), and no cell a flask can lie in. The flasks are placed when a round starts, so the sheep are the ones that yield: they keep out of the cells of `flaskCells` for the most flasks a round can have (`MAX_FLASK_COUNT`), and no flask ever moves because of a sheep. They are kept more than 4 passages apart while the maze has cells for that. A sheep that looks along the grid lies along a wall that runs the same way, 0.25 m towards the closed end. A sheep that looks along a diagonal stands across a closed corner, head at one wall and tail at the other. Each has one collision box, after the pillars and before the stile in `MazeWorld::colliders`, and no grass under it. It cannot be used, and the map does not show it.
Why: The model is a generated lying sheep, 0.75 m from nose to tail (`STONE_SHEEP_LENGTH`, was 0.9) and squeezed across to the 0.4 m of its box; widening the box instead broke the passage rules of the tests, see [generated-models](generated-models.md). I rejected the picture of the mossy wall for it: it draws masonry joints across the animal, so `stone_sheep.png` is its own generated picture of pale curled stone. I rejected a box around the whole sheep on a diagonal: it would be 0.68 m square and take the middle of the cell from the player. The box there is 0.48 m and holds the body, and the nose, the tail and the ears reach up to 0.11 m past it, towards the two walls, where the body of the player cannot follow.
Cost and revisit: Along a wall 1.3 m of the 1.7 m between the wall boxes stays free. Across a corner the way towards every open side keeps its full width, but beside the sheep itself only 1.16 m is free: in a dead end nobody passes there, and in a corner cell the walker turns through the other half of the cell. A corner cell has one closed corner, so it takes a sheep for six of the eight directions. With the crystals of the game the dead ends of the two small mazes are all taken, so their flock stands in corners, and in the largest maze about one sheep in forty has a dead end. The puddles the sheep avoid are the ones of the usual share, so the debug slider can put a puddle under one. The shade has no collision box and walks from the middle of a cell to the middle of the next, 0.43 m or more from every sheep box. In the cell of a player it sees, it walks straight at them and can brush a sheep. With the lamp off a sheep reads only where the moon reaches it. I reopen it when the sheep bells come: then a sheep may need a pick box.
