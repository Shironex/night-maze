# The start cell has a step stile in its north wall, with one collision box and bare ground
Status: accepted (2026-10-09)
Code: src/game/Stile.hpp, src/game/Stile.cpp, src/game/MazeWorld.cpp, src/game/MazeRenderer.cpp, src/game/Grass.cpp, src/game/WallVariants.cpp, src/game/GateLamp.cpp, tools/blender/build_stile.py, tests/StileTests.cpp

Context: The story says "the stile" for the place every night begins and where the shadow carries me back, and the start cell had nothing to show it. A player who was just carried back had nothing to recognise.
Decision: The north border wall of cell (0, 0) is drawn with `stile.obj`: four through-stones climb to a notch where the wall is 2.4 m high. `stile_post.obj` stands beside the notch, an oak post with an empty iron hook, because the lamp that hung there is in my hand. The steps and the post get one collision box, 1.66 m along the wall, from the box of the wall to 0.46 m into the cell and 1.92 m high, the last entry of `MazeWorld::colliders`. No grass grows in an oval in front of the steps and under them. The wall never gets a worn, crowned or broken look, and no milestone stands in the start cell. The stile is decoration: it cannot be climbed. The facing at the start is unchanged. Looking east I have the post at the left edge of the picture, a dark bar until I turn the lamp to it, and looking south the stile is behind me, where I came in.
Why: North and not west, because the wall model is not turned on a wall along X and its notch then lies towards the east, the side most starts look to. I rejected a new wall look for it: a look can be switched off in the debug window and is counted in the shares of the worn walls, and the box would stay behind an invisible stile. One index in the world, `stileWall`, tells the renderer, the collision list and the grass the same thing. The post reuses the picture of the gate, its plank for the oak and its iron band for the ring the hook is forged to, so there is no new picture.
Cost and revisit: The sketch put the post 0.93 m from the middle of the wall. The start cell is a corner, so there it stood inside the east wall, or in the mouth of the east passage without a box. It now stands at 0.75 m, in front of the far end of the notch, and the box reaches to it, 0.22 m longer than the steps. The box is as high above the low steps as above the top one. The player keeps 1.39 m of the 1.7 m between the wall boxes of the cell. The wood of the post below the band is stretched 3.7 times along the grain. I reopen it when inner stiles can be climbed: then the steps need boxes of their own.
