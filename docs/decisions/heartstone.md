# One heartstone a maze counts as three crystals, fills the battery and stops the sprint for 30 s
Status: accepted (2026-10-09)
Code: src/game/Heartstone.hpp, src/game/Heartstone.cpp, src/game/MazeWorld.cpp, src/game/Crystals.cpp, src/game/Round.cpp, src/game/Player.cpp, src/game/Puddles.cpp, src/game/SoundCues.cpp, src/debug/Hud.cpp, tools/blender/build_splinter.py, tools/make_sounds.py, tests/HeartstoneTests.cpp

Context: Every pickup was worth the same, so no dead end was worth a detour. The story has a line for a bigger one: "The big ones are the heart of a fall. Carry them slowly."
Decision: Every maze with a dead end to spare has one heartstone, in every mode and in a calm night: in the dead end farthest from the start by passages, which is never the exit or the cell before it. It adds 3 to what the player holds and fills the battery. The gate still counts its threshold from the ordinary crystals, so the heartstone is a shortcut and never needed, and "of N" grows by 3. For 30 s after it the sprint is refused. That is not winded: the bar refills and nobody breathes hard. A flask drunk in that time does not lift it, and the 20 s of its tea start when the weight is gone (`advanceStaminaClocks`). It has its own model, its own sound (cue 23) and a light of its own look in the list of the 16 nearest. The held map shows no mark for it and no note points at it.
Why: I rejected a weight per crystal in the list of the crystals. As one of them it would raise the threshold of the gate, get a diamond on the map and be a target of the hints, and adding a cell to their shuffle would move every crystal of every seed. It is a thing of its own beside them.
Cost and revisit: The walls, levers, notes, wall looks and flask order of a seed are what they were. In almost every maze of the three levels one crystal moves (117 of 120 measured), because the crystals fill the dead ends: the one that floated in that dead end, or in the dead end the flasks now take in its place. It goes to the next free cell of the crystals' order, which is usually not a dead end, and every other crystal keeps its cell and model. In about one round of twenty (6 of 120) one flask moves to the next dead end. Levers and puddles are still placed around the crystals of the seed (`MazeWorld::seedCrystals`), so none moves, and a puddle that would lie under the heartstone or under the moved crystal is left out. The camera of the menu follows the moved crystal. The 30 s and the 3 are untuned. I reopen it if players skip it, or if the far dead end next to the start feels unfair.
